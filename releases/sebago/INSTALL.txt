The Sebago field reader runs on your device.

Windows, macOS or Linux
Extract this ZIP. Open Sebago.html with a current browser such as Edge, Chrome,
Firefox or Safari. If opening the file shows its source, use your browser's Open
File command to open Sebago.html. This copy includes its model, fonts and photo.

Android or iOS
Use https://krummholz.ai/apps/sebago/#install on the phone for the offline web app.
On Android use Chrome's menu > Add to Home screen > Install.
On iOS use Safari's Share > Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App when
offered and tap Add. Open Sebago once while online and check the offline-ready
message before taking it into the field. App Store and Google Play listings are
planned for the coming weeks.

Choose a fictional field record, edit or hide a field, then click Read field.
Changes are stored in your local browser where it permits local storage. Clearing
browser data removes them. The Model card tab downloads the embedded weights.
Use separate field records and training code to retrain Sebago yourself. Krummholz
AI can retrain it for a small fee. See https://krummholz.ai/llms/sebago/.

Model and training code: https://huggingface.co/krummholz-ai/Sebago-40K
Field notes: https://krummholz.ai/field-notes/sebago-field-reader/

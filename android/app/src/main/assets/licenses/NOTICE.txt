Krummholz converts the original BF16 safetensors to GGUF with llama.cpp b11669.
Changes are Q/K tensor layout permutation and lossless F32 promotion of
normalization tensors. No quantization or Krummholz training is performed.
The model manifest and conversion report identify exact sources and hashes.
The model license does not establish public-domain status of inherited training
data. The current candidate remains unapproved pending application checks.
